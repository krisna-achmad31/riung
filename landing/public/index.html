How it works

Small practices, every day. The monsters slowly calm down.

A 5–15 minute daily ritual, grounded in CBT
(cognitive behavioral therapy).

01

Recognize

A relaxed ~5-minute quiz. You get your monster profile: Si Hakim as the boss, plus 6 minions ranked by how active they are.

02

Practice

Morning check-ins, micro-CBT missions, meditation, journaling or sleep stories. Every practice earns coins & tickets.

03

Tame

Use tickets to face monsters in mini-games. Even a loss still counts as progress — failing once doesn't mean "always failing".

FAQ

Frequently asked.

Is Riung therapy?

No. Riung is a CBT-based mental wellbeing tool. If things feel heavy, please reach out to a professional — and the crisis help screen is always available in the app.

Can anyone else read my journal?

No. Your journal is encrypted and stored on your phone, locked with a PIN or fingerprint. It's never sent to our servers, and cards you share never include what you wrote.

Do I have to pay to tame monsters?

No. Only practice moves monster progress — it can't be bought. Coins and Premium unlock extra content and cosmetics, never faster taming.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Check-ins, your journal, assessments and Focus Mode all work offline. Cloud backup is optional.
