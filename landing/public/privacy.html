Privacy Policy

Riung is a place for honest, sometimes heavy thoughts. This page explains, in plain words, what data the Riung app and this website handle, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version

  • Your journal and daily check-ins stay on your phone, and the journal is encrypted behind your own PIN. We never upload what you write.
  • You can use Riung without giving us your name or email.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.
  • The crisis help screen is always available and is never logged.
  • You can export your data or delete your account from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

"Riung", "we" and "us" mean the team that builds and runs the Riung mental wellbeing app for Android and the website at riung-5e979.web.app. This policy covers both. When you use Riung, we are the party responsible for your personal data under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and other laws that apply to you.

2. What we handle

DataWhat it is and why
Account IDWhen you first open Riung we create an anonymous account. It is a random ID, not your name. While you stay anonymous, your profile, progress and coins are kept only on your phone.
Email and name (optional)Only if you choose to sign in with email or Google, so you can continue on a new phone. Google sign-in shares your name, email and profile photo with us.
Profile and progress (signed-in accounts)Once you sign in, we sync your display name, chosen avatar, language, the result of the monster quiz (how active each monster is, 0 to 100%), streak, monster taming progress and Premium status, so they survive a phone change. 3. What stays on your phone

Some of the most personal parts of Riung never leave your device:

  • Journal entries are encrypted and stored only on your phone, locked with a separate PIN or biometrics.
  • Daily check-ins (mood, energy, sleep and your intention), your own affirmations and your favorites are stored only on your phone.
  • Frozen Apps. If you turn it on, Riung uses Android's Usage access permission to read how long the apps you chose are used, only to check the daily limit you set. If you also turn on the lock, Riung uses the Accessibility service only to know when one of your chosen apps is open, and brings you back to Riung once the limit has passed. Riung does not read screen content, keystrokes or passwords. Everything is processed on your phone, never sent to a server, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Sleep data from Health Connect. If you allow it, Riung reads your sleep sessions to show sleep and mood patterns in your reflection report. The data is read on your phone and is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Reports and personality results are calculated on your phone without AI, from check-ins and journal metadata (date, mood, tags), never from your journal text, and are not sent to a server.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We don't use push tracking.

Because these live only on your phone, uninstalling the app or losing the phone can remove them. Use Download all my data in Settings if you want a copy.

Riung's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We never use it for advertising, never sell it and never share it.

4. How we use it

  • To run the app: keep your account, save your progress, verify purchases and unlock what you paid for.
  • To keep the coin economy fair and protect it from tampering.
  • To understand which features help and fix what breaks, using analytics and crash reports.
  • To email you about the launch, if you joined the waitlist.

We process data because you asked us to provide the service (your agreement to the Terms of Service), because you gave consent (for example, Frozen Apps permissions, Health Connect or the waitlist), or because we have a legitimate interest in keeping Riung secure and working. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the feature or permission off.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use your journal, check-ins or quiz results to make automated decisions about you.

5. Who we share with

We don't sell or rent your personal data. We only share it with service providers who help us run Riung, and only as much as they need:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics, Hosting) stores account data and runs our servers.
  • Google Play processes payments and subscriptions.

These services may store data on servers outside Indonesia. Google protects it under its own security commitments and data processing terms. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, for example a valid court order.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting your account in Settings removes it immediately and permanently, together with the data on your phone. If you were offline and the cloud copy could not be removed, email us and we will remove it.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept for a limited period under Google's retention settings and are not linked to your name or email.
  • Waitlist emails are kept until Riung launches and we have told you, or until you ask us to remove yours.

7. Your rights

You can:

  • See and download your data with Download all my data in Settings.
  • Correct your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account and data with Delete account in Settings.
  • Withdraw consent by turning off Usage access, Accessibility, Health Connect or reminders at any time.
  • Ask us about your data, object to how we use it or complain, by emailing achmad.yukrisna@gmail.com. We will reply within 3 x 24 hours on working days, as required by Indonesian law.

8. Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and our servers. Your journal is encrypted on your phone. Your coin balance can only be changed by our server, and our database rules stop anyone from reading another person's account. No system is perfect, so if we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Age

Riung is made for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use Riung with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

10. The crisis help screen

The crisis help screen in Riung is always available, even without coins, Premium or an internet connection, and opening it is never logged or sent to analytics. If you need help now in Indonesia, call SEJIWA counseling at 119 ext. 8. If you are in danger, call 112.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app before they take effect.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data? Email achmad.yukrisna@gmail.com.
